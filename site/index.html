Unreinforced masonry (brick) buildings in Utah, by state and FEMA estimates. Most are homes.

Utah doesn't have a public earthquake early warning system yet. When it shakes, your preparation is the warning.

Magna was a warning shot

On March 18, 2020, a magnitude 5.7 earthquake rattled the Salt Lake Valley. It broke old brick, knocked out power, and gave scientists thousands of new recordings. What they learned about the valley's deep sediments changes how we think about the big one.
